We test how AI systems perform in real tasks.

OxRML is the Reasoning with Machines Lab at the University of Oxford. We study how large language models and AI agents reason, where they fail and how people use them.

Our work develops reliable ways to evaluate their capabilities and risks.

Research Themes

Our work focuses on three areas.

Benchmarks and Evaluation

We develop rigorous ways to assess what large language models can do and where current tests give misleading results.

Agentic AI for Science

We build AI agents for scientific knowledge synthesis and discovery, and test whether their results are reliable and transparent.

AI Safety and Security

We study risks including bias, toxicity and agentic misalignment, and develop methods to identify and address them.
