Building trust in the
next generation of AI.

As Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems become increasingly capable, society needs reliable ways to evaluate whether they are safe, trustworthy and ready for real-world use.

Led by Professor Adam Mahdi, the Reasoning with Machines Lab at the University of Oxford develops rigorous methods for evaluating AI, helping researchers, policymakers and industry make more informed decisions about the technologies shaping our future.

Our Research

Our research addresses some of the most important questions in AI evaluation.

Evaluating AI Systems

How can we accurately assess the capabilities of AI systems?

Developing rigorous methods and benchmarks to measure the performance, reliability and limitations of increasingly capable AI.

Understanding AI Behaviour

How do AI systems interact with people?

Studying how AI systems solve problems, communicate and collaborate with humans in real-world settings.

Building Trustworthy AI

How can we make AI systems safer, more reliable and transparent?

Generating the evidence needed for responsible AI development through empirical evaluation and scientific research.
