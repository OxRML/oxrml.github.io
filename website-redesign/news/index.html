September 2024 / Award

Winners of 2024 PhysioNet Challenge

Congratulations to Felix Krones, Ben Walker, Terry Lyons and Adam Mahdi on winning the 2024 George B. Moody PhysioNet Challenge by developing an algorithm that converts printed electrocardiograms into digital time-series data, unlocking historical medical records for analysis. The team achieved the top score on the hidden test set, with the award announced at Computing in Cardiology in Karlsruhe.

July 2024 / Conference

OxRML @ ICML 2024

Adam Mahdi and Andrew Bean organised the Data-Centric Machine Learning Research (DMLR) workshop at ICML 2024, Vienna, bringing together researchers to advance data-centric approaches to machine learning. The workshop focused on the role of datasets in foundation models, highlighting challenges in data quality, curation and evaluation and fostering discussion on how better data can drive more reliable and robust AI systems.
