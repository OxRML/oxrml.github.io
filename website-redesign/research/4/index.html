Evaluating the role of 'Constitutions' for learning from AI feedback

This study tests whether written behavioural rules, known as constitutions, improve AI responses to sensitive conversations. In an evaluation involving 215 human raters, more detailed rules improved some emotional qualities of responses. They did not outperform a baseline on the practical work of gathering or providing useful information.

Key result

More detailed constitutions improved some emotional response ratings, but none beat the baseline on practical information tasks.
