Improving in-context learning with small language model ensembles

This work combines predictions from several fine-tuned small language models to help a larger model learn from examples in its prompt. The ensemble improved results on general and medical classification tasks compared with the tested baselines. The findings suggest that smaller specialist models can supply useful guidance without retraining the larger model.

Key result

An ensemble of fine-tuned small models improved in-context classification on general and medical tasks.
