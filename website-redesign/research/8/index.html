2024 / Computing in Cardiology (CinC)

Combining Hough transform and deep learning approaches to reconstruct ECG signals from printouts

The researchers developed a system to recover digital electrocardiogram signals from images of printed traces. It combines image processing, including a Hough transform, with a U-Net model to locate and reconstruct the waveforms. The system won the 2024 PhysioNet Challenge on ECG digitisation, though its hidden-test score was lower than its cross-validation score.

Key result

The system ranked first in the 2024 PhysioNet ECG digitisation challenge, scoring 12.15 on the hidden test set.
