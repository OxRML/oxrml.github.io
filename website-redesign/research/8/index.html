2024 / Computing in Cardiology (CinC)

Combining Hough transform and deep learning approaches to reconstruct ECG signals from printouts

Paper electrocardiograms remain common and contain information that could be lost to digital analysis. This study combines a varied training set, Hough transform image rotation, U-Net signal segmentation and mask vectorisation to reconstruct digital traces from printouts. The method was assessed with ten-fold cross-validation on 21,799 recordings and in the 2024 PhysioNet Challenge. It achieved a mean cross-validation signal-to-noise ratio of 17.02 and scored 12.15 on the hidden digitisation test, placing first.

Key result

The ECG digitisation system placed first in the 2024 PhysioNet Challenge, with a hidden-test score of 12.15.
