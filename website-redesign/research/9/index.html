LINGOLY: A Benchmark of Olympiad-Level Linguistic Reasoning Puzzles in Low-Resource and Extinct Languages

LingOly tests language models on Olympiad-level linguistic reasoning problems drawn from more than 90 low-resource and extinct languages. The benchmark asks models to infer rules from examples rather than rely on common-language knowledge. Leading models improved when given relevant context, but still struggled with the hardest problems.

Key result

The strongest model solved 38.7 per cent of the hardest problems, 24.7 per centage points above its no-context baseline.
