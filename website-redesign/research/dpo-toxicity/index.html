How Does DPO Reduce Toxicity? A Mechanistic Neuron-Level Analysis

This study examines why direct preference optimisation reduces toxic language model outputs. The researchers traced a small part of the change to individual neurons associated with toxicity, while most of the effect came from broader shifts in model activations. Their results suggest that inspecting a few prominent neurons cannot fully explain how the training method changes behaviour.

Key result

Toxicity-linked neurons explained only 2.5 to 24 per cent of the reduction produced by direct preference optimisation.
