2022 / Computing in Cardiology (CinC)

Dual Bayesian ResNet: A deep learning approach to heart murmur detection

Heart murmur detection from recorded sounds can support screening for structural heart disease. This study converts heart sound recordings into overlapping spectrograms and uses a dual Bayesian residual network to classify murmurs. A second version combines the network output with patient details and signal features. The spectrogram model reached a weighted accuracy of 0.771 on the hidden PhysioNet Challenge test and ranked fourth. Adding patient information improved ordinary accuracy on a held-out subset but reduced weighted accuracy.

Key result

The spectrogram model ranked fourth in the 2022 PhysioNet murmur task with a hidden-test weighted accuracy of 0.771.
