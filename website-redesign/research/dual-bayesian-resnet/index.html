Dual Bayesian ResNet: A deep learning approach to heart murmur detection

This study uses Bayesian residual networks to detect heart murmurs from recorded sounds. The method placed fourth in the 2022 PhysioNet Challenge, with a hidden-test weighted accuracy of 0.771. Adding patient characteristics raised ordinary accuracy but reduced the challenge’s weighted score, illustrating a trade-off between evaluation measures.

Key result

The model ranked fourth in the 2022 PhysioNet Challenge with a weighted accuracy of 0.771.
