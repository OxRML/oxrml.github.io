2025 / Evaluating the Evolving LLM Lifecycle @ NeurIPS

Evaluating LLM-as-a-Judge under Multilingual, Multimodal and Multi-domain Constraints

Language models are increasingly used to judge other models during training, but their reliability across languages and images is uncertain. PolyVis evaluates vision-language judges in 12 languages on hallucination, safety, knowledge and reasoning tasks. The benchmark combines translated preference data with visual inputs so that task and language effects can be examined together. Judge performance changed with the language, task and model, showing that one aggregate score can hide important weaknesses in a particular setting.

Key result

Judge performance depended on the interaction of task, language and model across the 12-language PolyVis benchmark.
