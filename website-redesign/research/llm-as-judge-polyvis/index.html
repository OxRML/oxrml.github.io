2025 / Evaluating the Evolving LLM Lifecycle @ NeurIPS

Evaluating LLM-as-a-Judge under Multilingual, Multimodal and Multi-domain Constraints

PolyVis evaluates language models used to judge multilingual and multimodal outputs across tasks involving hallucination, safety, knowledge and reasoning. Tests in 12 languages show that judge performance depends on the combination of language, task and model. A single overall score can therefore conceal important failures in particular settings.

Key result

Judge accuracy varied across 12 languages and changed with the task and model being assessed.
