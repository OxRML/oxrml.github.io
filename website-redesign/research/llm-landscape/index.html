Do Large Language Models have Shared Weaknesses in Medical Question Answering?

The researchers compared 16 large language models on 874 Polish medical examination questions to understand where their answers overlap. Models with different overall scores still showed moderate agreement on which questions they answered correctly. The findings point to shared weaknesses that may persist even when several models are used together.

Key result

Pairwise agreement in question accuracy ranged from 0.39 to 0.58 across 16 models.
