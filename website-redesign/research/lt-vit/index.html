LT-ViT: A Vision Transformer for Multi-Label Chest X-Ray Classification

LT-ViT adapts a vision transformer for chest X-ray classification by allowing image tokens and disease label tokens to attend to one another. Tests on two public datasets found that it outperformed the pure vision transformer baselines used in the paper. Its label-aware attention also offers a direct way to inspect what supports each prediction.

Key result

LT-ViT outperformed the tested pure vision transformers on two public chest X-ray datasets.
