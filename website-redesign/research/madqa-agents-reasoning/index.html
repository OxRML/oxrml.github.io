Strategic Navigation or Stochastic Search? How Agents and Humans Reason Over Document Collections

Answering a question across a collection of PDFs demands more than finding a matching passage. MADQA tests multimodal agents with 2,250 human-written questions grounded in 800 varied documents and measures both accuracy and the effort needed to reach an answer. The strongest agents matched human searchers on overall accuracy, yet they often solved different questions. They relied on extensive trial and error, remained nearly 20 percentage points below an oracle with the right evidence, and sometimes continued searching without progress.

Key result

The best agents matched human accuracy but still fell nearly 20 percentage points short of an oracle, with inefficient searches exposing weak strategic planning.
