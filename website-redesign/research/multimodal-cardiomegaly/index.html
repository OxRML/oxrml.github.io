2022 / Annual Conference on Medical Image Understanding and Analysis (MIUA)

Multimodal cardiomegaly classification with image-derived digital biomarkers

The researchers assessed whether measurements derived from chest X-rays can classify an enlarged heart as effectively as models trained on the full image. A classifier using the cardiothoracic ratio and related measurements approached the accuracy of image-based and multimodal models. These simpler biomarkers offer a more transparent basis for prediction.

Key result

An interpretable biomarker classifier reached 81.2 per cent accuracy, close to 81.9 per cent for the strongest image-based approach.
