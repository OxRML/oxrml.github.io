2022 / Annual Conference on Medical Image Understanding and Analysis (MIUA)

Multimodal cardiomegaly classification with image-derived digital biomarkers

An enlarged heart can be detected from chest radiographs, but full-image models can be hard to interpret. This study estimates two image-derived measurements, the cardiothoracic ratio and cardiopulmonary area ratio, then compares a classifier using them with image-based and multimodal systems that also use intensive care data. The biomarker classifier reached 81.2 per cent accuracy, close to 81.9 per cent for the stronger full-image approaches. The comparable result offers a clearer clinical basis for a prediction.

Key result

A classifier using two image-derived biomarkers reached 81.2 per cent accuracy, close to the 81.9 per cent achieved by full-image systems.
