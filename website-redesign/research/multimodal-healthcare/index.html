2025 / Information Fusion

Review of multimodal machine learning approaches in healthcare

This review examines how machine learning systems combine medical images with other clinical data. Across more than 50 studies and 17 datasets, the authors compare fusion strategies, evaluation methods and practical limitations. The evidence shows that performance depends on the clinical task and available data, with no fusion method consistently best across settings.

Key result

The review found no single multimodal fusion method that consistently performed best across healthcare tasks.
