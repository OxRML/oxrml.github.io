2025 / Information Fusion

Review of multimodal machine learning approaches in healthcare

Clinical decisions draw on images together with patient history, laboratory results, vital signs and other records. This review examines how machine learning studies combine such sources rather than relying on a single modality. It surveys the data used in diagnosis, with particular attention to imaging, and compares fusion methods, training strategies and available multimodal datasets. The paper sets out the design choices and practical difficulties involved in building systems that reflect the wider clinical context of a patient.

Key result

The review maps how imaging and other clinical data are combined, including fusion methods, datasets and training choices.
