Meet the Team

The Reasoning with Machines Lab brings together researchers united by a shared commitment to understanding and evaluating increasingly capable AI systems. Combining mathematical rigour with empirical investigation, the team explores how AI systems reason, solve problems and interact with people.

Lab Lead

Professor Adam Mahdi

Associate Professor, University of Oxford

Adam Mahdi is an Associate Professor and Senior Research Fellow at the Oxford Internet Institute, and an associate member of St Antony’s College, Oxford. He was a Fellow of Wolfson College and a Stipendiary Lecturer at Lady Margaret Hall, Oxford.
